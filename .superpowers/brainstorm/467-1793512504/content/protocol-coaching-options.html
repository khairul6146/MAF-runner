Option B: Split-Lap Degradation Test

Interval Benchmark

Splits the benchmark into 3 equal timed or distance laps (e.g. 3x 1-mile or 3x 10-minute splits).

• Fatigue Tracking: In MAF testing, runners must reduce treadmill speed on subsequent laps to maintain a steady HR.
• Decoupling Metric: Calculates the speed degradation % between Lap 1 and Lap 3.
• Post-Run: Detailed split breakdown and cardiac recovery rate.
